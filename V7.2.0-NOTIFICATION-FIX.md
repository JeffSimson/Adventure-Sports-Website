# V7.2.0 Notification Reliability Fix

- Fixes notifications not appearing while the iPhone Home Screen app is open.
- Attaches the Firebase foreground-message listener only after Firebase and the service worker are initialized.
- Displays an iOS notification banner manually for foreground messages.
- Notification delivery is independent from Visibility & Sending Power controls. Those controls only affect in-app visibility and who may send alerts.
- Keeps fresh-token enrollment and per-device Netlify diagnostics from V7.1.9.

After deployment, each device should open the Home Screen app once. Re-enrollment is not normally required unless the device is missing from the enrolled list.
