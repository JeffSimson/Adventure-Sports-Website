ADVENTURE SPORTS V7.3.2 — INTERNAL MANAGEMENT SECTIONS + MOBILE POLISH

WHAT CHANGED
- Removed the Daily Operations Checklist from the Operations Center dashboard.
- Added cleaner, more even spacing and card sizing on phones.
- Added role-based internal sections inside the tabs that contain management controls.

GAMES
- Regular users with Games access see Games & Fields only.
- Owners and Managers also see Tournament Management.
- Tournament Matrix Manager is completely absent for Grounds, Kitchen, Cashier, and other non-management roles.

NOTIFICATIONS
- Regular users see Notification History and their device enable/refresh controls.
- Send Notification, recipient targeting, device administration, visibility rules, and sending permissions are moved into Notification Management.
- Notification Management is visible to Owners only.
- Managers and regular staff do not see notification permission or sending controls.

PEOPLE & PERMISSIONS
- Employee Directory remains the simple daily section.
- Roles, permissions, owners, notification access, and audit information are moved into a separate Roles & Permissions section visible to Owners only.
- Managers can access allowed employee operations without seeing role/permission administration.

WEBSITE
- Website publishing controls are grouped under Website Management inside the Website tab.

WEATHER
- Field-status management is hidden from non-management roles.
- Owners and Managers retain the management section inside Weather.

SECURITY / VISIBILITY
- Restricted sections are removed from the visible interface instead of showing disabled controls or permission explanations.
- Existing backend role checks remain in place.
