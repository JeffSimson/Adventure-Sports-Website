How pricing works

Admission supports the event experience.

Adventure Sports works with outside programmers who determine the admission cost for spectators. Pricing is based on a model designed to help Adventure Sports cover approximately 75% of event-related cleaning, facility repair, and security costs.

For most tournaments, spectator admission typically ranges from $10 to $20. When Adventure Sports directly runs an event, admission is typically discounted by approximately 30% because projected operating costs are lower.

Good to know: Final admission pricing can vary by event. Admission is collected at the facility on the day of the event.

Spectator conduct

Help us keep game day safe, respectful, and clean.

These expectations apply to all spectators and guests while on Adventure Sports property.

Respect

No heckling or harassment

Do not heckle, harass, or aggressively confront players, coaches, officials, staff, or other spectators.

Safety

No fighting or threats

Fighting, threatening behavior, or conduct that creates an unsafe environment is not permitted.

Facility

No littering or damage

Do not litter, damage facility property, remove equipment, or steal Adventure Sports property.

Awareness

Watch the field at all times

Remain in designated spectator areas and behind protective netting whenever available. Stay alert for balls or equipment entering spectator areas.
