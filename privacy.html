Privacy Policy

Last updated August 4, 2026

The Adventure Sports Operations Hub is a private workforce and facility-management system for authorized Adventure Sports & Entertainment team members. This policy explains the information used by the Operations Hub and how it supports facility, safety, and game-day operations.

Information we process

The system may process account name and email, assigned role and permissions, login and security events, device and browser details used for push-notification enrollment, operational records entered by authorized users, tournament and game-day changes, work orders, incident records, and audit history.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, enforce role-based access, deliver requested operational alerts, run game-day and facility workflows, maintain business and safety records, diagnose service problems, prevent misuse, and protect the Operations Hub.

Push notifications

When a user enables notifications, the system stores a protected device registration token and basic device information needed to route alerts. Users or authorized Owners can rename or remove enrolled devices and control notification categories and quiet hours. Notification providers may process delivery information to send the alert.

Role-based access

Information is shown according to the user’s approved role and permissions. Sensitive owner, security, incident, database, employee, and delivery information is restricted to authorized roles. Users should not share accounts or allow another person to use an unlocked signed-in device.

Service providers

The Operations Hub relies on service providers used to host the website, authenticate users, store operational data, deliver push notifications, provide weather information, and connect approved business services. Those providers process information only as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy terms.

Retention and security

Records are retained according to operational, safety, employment, accounting, and legal needs. The system uses authentication, protected roles, audit logging, encrypted connections, session controls, and restricted administrative tools. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Choices and requests

Authorized users can disable push notifications in their device settings or remove an enrolled device from the Operations Hub. Questions about access, correction, retention, or deletion requests should be sent to the support contact below. Some records may need to be retained for business, safety, or legal reasons.

Contact

Email support@adventurenj.com or use the Adventure Sports contact page.
